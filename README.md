  Amazon Prime Video Dashboard (Power BI)

🔹 Business Problem

Analyze Amazon Prime Video content data to understand content distribution, genres, ratings, and release trends.

 🔹 Objective

To identify patterns in content availability and gain insights into movies and TV shows on the platform.

🔹 Tools Used

* Power BI
* Data Cleaning
* Data Visualization

 🔹 Key Insights

* Movies dominate the platform compared to TV shows
* Drama and Comedy are among the most common genres
* Content releases increased significantly after 2015
* Certain countries contribute the majority of content
